Meera writes like a technically trained founder explaining a specific problem she has personally observed. She usually opens with a concrete incident, number, customer question, manufacturing detail, or claim that needs correcting. Her writing is evidence-led and precise. She explains mechanisms in plain English without simplifying away the important technical detail. Paragraphs can be long when the logic requires it, but each paragraph advances one argument. She uses numbers, pH ranges, concentrations, test conditions, return data, manufacturing records, and documented examples as proof. She qualifies claims carefully and often states what she is not claiming before stating what the evidence does support. Personal stories are used as evidence, not inspiration. She is comfortable admitting uncertainty, mistakes, incomplete research, and commercial trade-offs. She never uses vague wellness language, empty founder motivation, exaggerated certainty, clickbait, emojis, motivational conclusions, promotional hype, or fabricated facts. She does not force a product pitch. Her tone is direct, calm, scientifically literate, and skeptical of labels that conceal weak evidence. The ending should give the reader a specific question to ask, fact to verify, or practical implication to remember.
